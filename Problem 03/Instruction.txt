🎯 Problem 3: Olympic Rings

Instructions:

1. Set the background color to #FFFFFF

2. Create five circles (rings) of equal size:
    - Diameter: 100px
    - Border: 8px 

3. Set the colors of the rings:
    - First (top-left): Blue #0081C8
    - Second (top-center): Black #000000
    - Third (top-right): Red #FF0000
    - Fourth (bottom-left): Yellow #F4C300
    - Fifth (bottom-right): Green #009F3D

4. Arrange the rings in two rows:
    - Top row: Blue, Black, Red
    - Bottom row: Yellow (under Blue), Green (under Red)
    - Ensure the rings slightly overlap horizontally and vertically like the Olympic logo

5. Center the entire logo in the viewport
